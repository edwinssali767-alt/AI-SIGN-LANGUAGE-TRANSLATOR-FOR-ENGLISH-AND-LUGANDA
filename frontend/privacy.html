Privacy and camera use

This translator needs your camera to see your hands. Here is exactly what happens with it.

Video is never stored

We do not record, save or keep video or photos from your camera. Nothing you sign is kept as a picture.

Signs to sentence runs on your device

The “Signs to sentence” panel finds your hand and works out the sign inside your browser. No camera image leaves your phone or computer, and it keeps working without an internet connection after the first visit.

The older Capture and Real-time buttons

These send one frame at a time to this server so it can name the sign. The frame is processed in memory and thrown away. Only the resulting word is saved in your translation history, if you are signed in.

Helping to improve the translator

Administrators can record sign examples to train the translator. Only a list of hand-point coordinates is saved, never video or photos, and only after the signer ticks a consent box. A signer is identified by a code, not a name. Ask an administrator to remove your recordings at any time.

Speech input

If you use “Speak instead” on the Text to Sign page, your browser may send the audio to its own speech service. Typing never leaves your device.

Your choices

You can turn the camera off at any time with the Stop button, and withdraw camera permission in your browser settings.
